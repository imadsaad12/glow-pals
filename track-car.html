Questions? We've got answers.

How does the line-following work?

Draw a track on paper (or any light surface) and place the car on the line. The infrared sensors underneath read the line and steer the car along it — every curve, loop and zigzag.

Do I need a special marker?

Nope! Any regular marker or thick-tipped pen works. Darker and thicker lines work best.

What play modes does it have?

Four! 🛣️ Track mode (follows lines they draw), 🖐️ Gesture mode (follows their hand or runs away when chased), ⚽ Ball mode (chases and dribbles the included ball) and 🎮 classic Remote Control mode.

How does it charge?

The car recharges with the included USB-C cable — no batteries to buy or replace for the car itself. Plug it in, let it top up, and it's ready for the next race.

Is it durable?

Very. The reinforced, impact-resistant shell is built to survive crashes into walls, tumbles off tables and everything in between.
